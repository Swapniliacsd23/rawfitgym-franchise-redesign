06GOOD TO KNOW

QUESTIONS
IN MIND?

Answers follow the franchise information published by RAW FIT GYM.

01What is the minimum space required?

RAW FIT Prime is planned for 3,000–4,000 sq. ft. and Luxury for 6,000–8,000 sq. ft. Final requirements depend on site measurement, structural review and equipment planning.

02What is the investment?

The indicative franchise investment is ₹1.80 Crore for Prime and ₹3.20 Crore for Luxury. Figures are indicative and subject to final commercial agreement.

03Can I use my own property?

Yes. A suitable owned property can be evaluated against visibility, access, catchment, structure, services and lease criteria. The team says it can also support site evaluation if you do not have a property.

04Is IFBB Pro Rahul included?

Any endorsement, ambassador role, appearance schedule, image rights or commercial association is subject to a separate written agreement. Athlete involvement is not guaranteed as part of the franchise.

05How is the format chosen?

The brand says format selection considers your property, catchment profile and investment range. The team evaluates the site and recommends Prime or Luxury accordingly.

06What happens next?

Share your city, property size and investment range through the enquiry form. The RAW FIT team can discuss suitability, format and next steps.
